Tips For Success

✓ Warm up for 5 minutes: Light cardio or dynamic stretches to prepare your body.
✓ Focus on form over speed: Quality movements prevent injuries and maximize results.
✓ Rest 48 hours: Allow muscles time to recover and rebuild between intense sessions.
✓ Progress gradually: Add reps or difficulty every 2-3 weeks for continued gains.
